Our Services

Equipped with advanced machinery, our production line is capable of processing virtually any type of stone: marble, granite, quartz, ceramics, and any other engineered stone surfaces.
Section 1: For Developers & Main Contractors (B2B)

Commercial Scale Fabrication

High-capacity CNC processing and automated cutting lines designed to handle high-volume slab fabrication for residential towers, hotels, and commercial developments without compromising on deadlines or quality.

Compliance & Project Support

Full documentation support for green building requirements. Our materials carry global NSF, Greenguard, and RoHS 2.0 certifications, ensuring seamless approvals by local authorities and consultants in the UAE.

On-Time Turnkey Delivery

Dedicated project management from strict laser site-surveys to final structural installation. We align seamlessly with your master construction schedule to prevent any site delays.

Section 2: For Architects & Interior Designers (B2B)

Premium Material & Texture Selection

Access to an extensive collection of elite quartz surfaces, including complex Calacatta patterns. We offer ideal aesthetics, thickness options, and finishes to flawlessly match your architectural vision.

Flawless Bookmatching & Vein Alignment

Advanced digital slab mapping allows us to align intricate veins perfectly across continuous countertops, waterfalls, and feature walls, preserving the artistic integrity of your design.

Bespoke Edge Profiling & Detailing

High-precision custom edge crafting (mitred joints under 45 degrees, complex profiles) executed on advanced Italian-grade machinery to fulfill the most ambitious architectural concepts.

Section 3: For Private Clients & Homeowners (B2C) Turnkey
Up to 10 Years Warranty
✨ Full turnaround from measurement to final installation with up to 10 years warranty on stone and a 1-year warranty on workmanship.

Hassle-Free "White-Glove" Service

A complete end-to-end solution for your villa or apartment. We take care of everything: from professional home consultation with material samples and laser measurements to clean, careful delivery and final installation. All we need is your approval.

Hygiene & Ultimate Family Safety

Non-porous, bacteria-resistant stone structure officially certified by NSF for safe food contact. Perfect for family kitchens and bathrooms — the material doesn't absorb odors, requires no chemical sealing, and is completely safe for children.

Lifetime Durability & Easy Care

Engineered to withstand daily life. Our premium quartz is highly scratch, stain, and heat-resistant against coffee, wine, and warm dishes. A simple wipe with a damp cloth instantly returns it to its pristine, luxury look.
