About Us

Welcome to Micaron Quartz — Your Expert for Premium Quartz Surfaces in the UAE.

At Micaron Quartz, we believe that quality is the foundation of every space, whether it is a private residence or a large-scale commercial project. We are the official authorized representative of a leading world-class factory producing premium-grade quartz slabs. Our mission is to bring a blend of luxury, exceptional reliability, and cutting-edge technology to interiors across the UAE and the GCC region.

  • Certified Quality & Safety: All our products are officially certified to international standards, including NSF, Greenguard, and RoHS 2.0, confirming the environmental purity and safety of our materials.
  • Direct Supply: As an official factory representative, we guarantee premium quality at competitive prices, providing direct supply channels across the UAE and GCC.
  • Full-Cycle Service: We do more than just supply stone; we provide a comprehensive turnkey solution. Our experts handle the full cycle of services: professional measurements, high-precision cutting, and expert installation.
  • Reliability & Durability: Our surfaces are incredibly durable, resistant to daily wear and tear, and designed to maintain their pristine appearance for years to come.
  • The Perfect Choice: Our extensive collection offers the perfect color and texture to bring any design vision to life.
  • Easy Maintenance: We value your time. Our quartz surfaces are easy to maintain, making them the ideal choice for modern living.

Located in the heart of Dubai’s industrial sector (Dubai Investment Park), we take pride in offering comprehensive solutions for designers, architects, and private clients. From professional consultation to final installation, Micaron Quartz is your trusted partner.

Our Services

Equipped with advanced machinery, our production line is capable of processing virtually any type of stone: marble, granite, quartz, ceramics, and any other engineered stone surfaces.

Commercial Scale Fabrication

High-capacity CNC processing and automated cutting lines designed to handle high-volume slab fabrication for residential towers, hotels, and commercial developments without compromising on deadlines or quality.

Compliance & Project Support

Full documentation support for green building requirements. Our materials carry global NSF, Greenguard, and RoHS 2.0 certifications, ensuring seamless approvals by local authorities and consultants in the UAE.

Dedicated project management from strict laser site-surveys to final structural installation. We align seamlessly with your master construction schedule to prevent any site delays.

Premium Material & Texture Selection
